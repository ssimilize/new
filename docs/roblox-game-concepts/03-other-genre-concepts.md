# Part 3 — Concepts for Other Genres

Multiple options per genre, each in a compact format. Genres are ordered by how well they fit the research in [Part 1](01-research.md) (trend strength × retention × fit for a small team with GUI skills).

Legend: **Scope** S = a solo dev can ship an MVP in ~1–2 months · M = small team, ~3 months · L = bigger team or 4+ months.

---

## A. Incremental / Clicker (GUI-heavy)

*Why:* under-served on Roblox for *polished* entries; the audience expects depth (Antimatter Dimensions, Prestige Tree). Pure GUI = cheap to build. Weakness: needs a social layer to break out.

### A1. **Singularity Factory** — S/M
> *"Start with one atom. End by building a universe."*
- Layers: Atoms → Molecules → Stars → Galaxies → Universes, each a new prestige layer with its own mechanic (not just a bigger number).
- Automation unlocks ("Robots") remove earlier-layer clicking as you progress.
- **Social:** a shared "Cosmos" in the hub showing every player's universe as a floating planet; server-wide "Big Bang" event when the combined server progress hits a goal.
- **Money:** Auto-buyer pass, 2× offline, cosmetic planet skins.

### A2. **Upgrade Tree Tycoon** — S
> *"One giant skill tree. Every node changes the rules."*
- Prestige-Tree-like: a huge zoomable tree; nodes add mechanics (e.g. "clicks now also generate rain that waters crops").
- Weekly **community node**: the server votes which new branch is added next update.
- **Money:** respec tokens (earnable too), tree themes.

### A3. **Button Wars** — S
> *"Everybody shares one giant button. Who gets the credit?"*
- A physical 3D button in the hub plus GUI incremental meta. Pressing contributes to a server-wide counter; milestones trigger events. Personal upgrades make your presses worth more.
- Teams (Red/Blue) race to server milestones → strong co-play signal.
- **Money:** press-power passes (team-balanced), trails and press effects.

## B. Obby × Incremental hybrids (the "+1 Speed" wave)

*Why:* +1 Speed Keyboard Escape proved "number goes up → reach further" is the hottest pattern of 2026. Short to build, strong for kids, great for YouTube/TikTok.

### B1. **+1 Jump Height Tower** — S
> *"Every second you get +1 jump. Climb the endless sky tower."*
- Jump grows passively (idle) and faster when you collect orbs. Higher floors = new biomes and orb multipliers. Rebirth for permanent jump multiplier.
- **Social:** race mode with friends; troll-safe (no pushing).
- **Money:** 2× jump gain pass, wing cosmetics, rewarded ad for a 5-min boost.

### B2. **Grow Big Escape** — S
> *"Eat to grow. Grow to smash through walls. Escape the giant house."*
- Your character's size grows +1 per second; bigger = can break bigger obstacles (visual, satisfying physics).
- **Social:** co-op "carry" — big players can carry small friends.
- **Money:** growth multipliers, skins; limited-time "giant" events.

### B3. **Magnet Run** — S
> *"Your magnet gets stronger every second — pull coins, then pull whole buildings."*
- Strength idles up; world objects have strength requirements. Rebirths change the world theme (city → space).
- **Money:** magnet skins, strength boosts.

## C. Tycoon / Steal-and-Defend

*Why:* fastest-growing category after Steal a Brainrot; idle income + social risk + memes. Weakness: meme timing is luck; PvP stealing frustrates some players — offer protection options.

### C1. **Dragon Hoard** — M
> *"Hatch dragons, build a hoard, raid other lairs — but guard your eggs."*
- Dragons generate gold idle; rare dragons are worth stealing. Traps and guard dragons defend. Stealing is a short, fun chase — not griefing (stolen items return a portion on logout; shields after being robbed).
- **Money:** lair shields (time-limited, also earnable), trap cosmetics, egg luck (odds shown).

### C2. **Museum Heist Tycoon** — M
> *"Build the world's best museum. Other players will try to rob it."*
- Collect artifacts (collection hook), display them for idle visitor income. Night phase: other players can attempt heists with gadgets; you build security (lasers, guards).
- **Social:** co-op heist crews.
- **Money:** security gadget passes, exhibit themes.

### C3. **Food Truck Empire** — S/M *(no-PvP variant)*
> *"Start with one taco truck, end with a food-truck city."*
- Classic tycoon + "customers" from other players' trucks: players physically visiting your truck gives bonus income (co-play reason without stealing).
- **Money:** chef hires, truck skins, 2× cash.

## D. Tower Defense × Collection RPG

*Why:* Anime Vanguards / Anime Last Stand show strong, sticky audiences. Crowded with anime clones — **an original, non-anime theme is the differentiator** (and avoids IP risk).

### D1. **Toybox Defense** — M
> *"Your toys come alive at night to defend the bedroom from the Dust Bunnies."*
- Collect toy units (army men, dinosaurs, robots), merge duplicates to upgrade. Maps = rooms of a house. Co-op 4-player waves.
- **Money:** unit banners with shown odds (all units earnable via drops), battle pass, toy skins.

### D2. **Mythic Gate Defense** — M
> *"Summon gods and monsters from world myths to hold the gate."*
- Norse, Greek, Egyptian, Aztec, Japanese folklore units — public-domain myths, huge roster potential, educational appeal.
- Idle "Garrison" mode: units earn resources while offline.
- **Money:** banners, evolution materials packs, cosmetics.

### D3. **Kitchen Chaos TD** — S/M
> *"Defend the cake from the invading ants."*
- Cute food-themed units; very readable for young players; quick 5-minute matches (mobile-friendly).

## E. Co-op Survival / Horror

*Why:* 99 Nights in the Forest and Forsaken show co-op survival with progression is a breakout lane. Weakness: horror retention is lower (D30 ~2.8%) unless RPG progression is added.

### E1. **99 Floors Below** — M
> *"Descend an abandoned research lab one floor per night. Scavenge, build, survive."*
- 1–6 player co-op; each floor adds a new threat; scavenge and craft between floors. Classes (Medic, Engineer, Scout) with between-run progression.
- **Money:** class unlocks (also earnable), flashlight/cosmetic packs, revive tokens (co-op only).

### E2. **Lighthouse Keepers** — M
> *"Keep the light burning till dawn. Something in the fog hates the light."*
- Resource management co-op (fuel, repairs, rowing to islands). Slow-burn dread, system mastery > reflexes (what made 99 Nights work).

### E3. **The Night Shift** — S/M
> *"Your first job at a haunted convenience store. Follow the rules."*
- Rule-based horror (a popular internet format): each night new rules; break one and things go wrong. Short sessions, great for streamers.

---

## F. Action / Anime-style RPG (original IP)

*Why:* One-Piece-inspired RPGs dominate, but they're L-scope and IP-risky. Original worlds with the same *progression feel* are the lane.

### F1. **Sky Pirates** — L
> *"Sail floating islands, find Wind Cores that grant powers, build your crew."*
- Same fantasy as Blox Fruits (explore → power up → bosses) with an original "Wind Core" power system and ship-building.
- **Money:** core rerolls (odds shown), ship skins, crew slots.

### F2. **Elemental Academy** — M/L
> *"Enroll at a magic school, master one of 12 elements, climb the rankings."*
- School-year structure (terms = seasons), classes = dungeons, exams = boss raids. Normalized-stat duels for PvP fairness.

---

## G. Social / Roleplay / Creative

*Why:* Brookhaven and Dress to Impress show social games hold huge audiences; strong D7 thanks to friend groups. Weakness: hard to monetize without great cosmetics.

### G1. **Design My Room** — M
> *"Each round has a theme. Decorate a room. Everyone votes."*
- Dress-to-Impress format applied to interior design. Unlock furniture through play; your saved rooms become your personal hangout.

### G2. **Pet Café** — M
> *"Run a café with your friends' pets as staff."*
- Roleplay + light tycoon: players take roles (barista, cashier, customer), pets help. Voice-chat friendly.

---

## H. Party / Minigame

*Why:* "Best Party Game" is now a Roblox award category; co-play is a new discovery signal. Weakness: needs constant new minigames.

### H1. **Floor Is Lava Show** — S/M
> *"A game-show host throws a new rule every 60 seconds."*
- Round-based minigame rotation with a sassy AI host; lots of small minigames built from a shared framework.

### H2. **Tiny Olympics** — M
> *"Silly physics sports: sumo, hammer throw, wobbly sprint."*
- Ragdoll physics, 8-player lobbies, season leaderboards.

---

## I. Strategy / Roguelike (GUI-first)

*Why:* strongly GUI-friendly, aimed at the growing 13–24 audience, very low competition on Roblox.

### I1. **Tiny Kingdoms** — M
> *"A 15-minute strategy match with 6 friends. Build, trade, betray."*
- Board-game-style GUI (Catan / Risk-lite) over a 3D table with everyone's avatars sitting around it — the social hook.

### I2. **Merchant Roads** — M
> *"Buy low, sell high, survive the road."*
- Caravan trading roguelike: GUI market screens, random road events, idle caravans once you own routes.

### I3. **Idle Deckbuilder Arena** — see Concept 6 in [Part 2](02-idle-rpg-concepts.md#concept-6--dungeon-deckmaster-idle-idle-deck-builder-rpg).

## Cross-genre summary

| Genre | Trend strength | Retention | Small-team fit | Best pick |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Idle RPG** (Part 2) | High | Highest (RPG + sim) | Good | Monster Ranch / Guildhall |
| Obby × Incremental | Very high (2026 wave) | Low–medium | Excellent (S) | B1 +1 Jump Height Tower |
| Tycoon / Steal-and-Defend | Very high | Medium–high | Medium | C1 Dragon Hoard |
| Tower Defense RPG | High | High | Medium | D1 Toybox Defense |
| Co-op Survival Horror | High | Low (medium with RPG) | Medium | E1 99 Floors Below |
| Incremental (GUI) | Niche | Medium–high | Excellent | A1 Singularity Factory |
| Action RPG | High | High | Poor (L) | F2 Elemental Academy |
| Social / Creative | High | Medium–high (D7) | Medium | G1 Design My Room |
| Party | Rising | Low–medium | Medium | H1 Floor Is Lava Show |
| Strategy / Roguelike | Niche | Medium | Good | I1 Tiny Kingdoms |

**Portfolio suggestion:** build one fast **S-scope hit-attempt** (e.g. *+1 Jump Height Tower*, 3–6 weeks) to learn Roblox's discovery system and build a player group, *while* developing the main **idle RPG** (Monster Ranch or Guildhall). Cross-promote the idle RPG to the audience the small game gathers.
