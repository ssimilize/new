01

Pitch & design pillars

One-sentence pitch: Hatch monsters, raise them on your ranch, send them on expeditions while you sleep.

Monster Ranch Idle combines the two idle patterns proven at the very top of Roblox. From Grow a Garden it takes real-time growth that continues offline, plus server-wide weather that creates rare mutations. From Adopt Me and Pet Simulator it takes collectible creatures with a trading economy. On top sits the RPG layer those games lack: squads of monsters that battle through regions, evolve along different paths, and breed into hybrids nobody on the server has discovered yet.

P1Always something ready

Every return has a payoff: a full coin jar, a hatched egg, a finished expedition, a monster ready to evolve. Timers run in parallel, so a player is never stuck waiting on just one.

P2Your ranch is on display

Every ranch is a real plot on a shared server. Other players walk past your Golden Blazefang and your Hall of Fame statues. Rare monsters give you status only because people can see them.

P3The server shares moments

Egg restocks, weather, the Stampede boss and discovery broadcasts happen to everyone on the server at once. They give players something to talk about and a reason to play together.

P4Choices change the monster

Diet decides the evolution branch. Breeding pairs decide hybrids and traits. Which pen a monster stands in during a storm decides its mutation. Two players with the same egg end up with different monsters.

P5Fair to kids, generous to free players

No paid timer skips, no punishment for neglect, odds shown on every random item. Mythics can be earned for free. Players pay for convenience, parallel slots, cosmetics and server-wide fun.

P6Content is data

Species, eggs, regions, weather and events are rows in config tables built on a modular monster kit. A new species or region ships as data and art, with no new systems code.

Stats, care and traits

Stats

HP, ATK, DEF, SPD for battle, plus Luck for expedition drops.

stat = base[species] × rarityMult × (1 + 0.06 × (lv − 1)) × starMult × traitMods

Star multiplier: 1★ 1.00, 2★ 1.25, 3★ 1.60, 4★ 2.00, 5★ 2.60. Stars come from fusing a duplicate of the same species and rarity, or from Star Shards.

Mood (care without punishment)

Mood runs from 0 to 100 and changes coin output between −10% and +10%. Feeding, petting from visitors and decor auras raise it. It drops slowly while you play and freezes while you're offline. Monsters never get sick, run away or die.

Kids shouldn't come back to a punishment. Care is an upside, never a tax.

Every monster rolls 2 visible traits at birth and 1 hidden trait that is revealed when it becomes an Adult. There are 20 traits at launch. A sample:

Variants, mutations and weight

Three separate collectible layers. They multiply together, and they are what drives trading and bragging.

Variant (on hatch)

Normal · Golden 1 in 40 (×3 coins) · Rainbow 1 in 400 (×10 coins). Rolled for every egg.

Mutation (from weather)

Up to 2 per monster, from weather while the monster stands in a pen. Some pairs combine into rarer ones. See Weather.

Weight

Rolled from 0.5× to 2× the species' average weight. Anything over 1.8× is Huge (1 in 100, ×2 value) and appears on the server's heaviest-monster board.

Only monsters, eggs and some cosmetics can be traded. Currency is never tradable. That removes the main channel for real-money trading and scam laundering.

Core formulas (starting values, tune with telemetry)

-- Coin income per monster, per second
coinsPerSec = baseRate[rarity]          -- 1, 3, 8, 25, 80, 250
            × stageMult[stage]          -- 0.4 baby, 0.7 teen, 1.0 adult
            × 1.07 ^ (level - 1)        -- Lv 50 ≈ ×27.5
            × starMult[stars]           -- 1.0, 1.25, 1.6, 2.0, 2.6
            × variantMult               -- 1, Golden 3, Rainbow 10
            × Π mutationMult            -- max 2 mutations
            × (0.9 + mood / 500)        -- 0.90 .. 1.10
            × (1 + legacyBonus[element] + friendBoost)

-- Level-up cost
levelCoins(lv)  = floor(50 × 1.12 ^ lv)
levelTreats(lv) = ceil(lv / 5)

-- Offline: each pen's jar fills to its cap; timers and expeditions finish normally
jar = min(jar + penRate × elapsed, penRate × jarCapSeconds)

-- Expedition loot per minute on a cleared stage
lootPerMin = regionBase × 1.08 ^ stage × durationBonus   -- 15m ×1.00 · 1h ×1.05 · 4h ×1.10 · 8h ×1.20

Expeditions & combat

This is the RPG half of the game, built the way AFK-style idle RPGs work. Stage battles push you forward. Timed expeditions farm your highest cleared stage while you're away.

Battle rules

Auto-battle

  • A squad of 3 (4 from Lv 35) fights enemy waves. The front slot draws 50% of enemy attacks and the other slots 25% each.
  • Turn order goes by SPD. Each species has a Basic attack, a Special that charges over 3 turns, and a Passive.
  • Battles are capped at 30 turns. If time runs out, the stage is lost.

dmg = ATK × power × elementMult × 100 / (100 + DEF) × rand(0.9, 1.1)

Watch or skip

  • Stage battles play as a 15-second replay with big numbers. Tap to skip.
  • Cheer: tapping during a replay fills a meter that gives +20% damage. Players who want to be active can be; the result is the same for those who skip.
  • The server simulates the battle deterministically from a seed, and the client only replays the result.

Timed expeditions

  • Send a squad to your highest cleared stage for 15 min, 1 h, 4 h or 8 h. Longer runs pay a bonus, up to ×1.2 for 8 h, so players are rewarded for going to bed with an expedition running.
  • Loot: coins, food, Treats, XP, and a chance of an egg (Meadow and Grove early; Crystal from Ember Peaks on).
  • Slots: 1 at start, 2 at Lv 6, 3 at Lv 15, +1 with the Explorer pass. Monsters on an expedition leave their pen and stop producing coins, which is a real trade-off.
  • Caravans (co-op): 2–4 players on the same server launch a Caravan together (4 h or 8 h). Everyone gets +30% loot and a shared Caravan Chest with a guaranteed Grove Egg.

Weather & mutations

Weather is shared by the whole server and is the main source of rare, tradable value. Every 20 minutes the server rolls weather that lasts 4 minutes. Monsters standing in a pen during that time can mutate. That turns a boring question, which monsters go in the pens, into a real decision and a reason to log in at the right moment.

Combo mutations

Some pairs merge into a rarer mutation that takes a single slot: Soaked + Frosted → Frozen (×3.5) and Scorched + Charged → Plasma (×4). Players will figure these out and share them, which is free marketing.

Weather Totems

A Robux product that summons Rain, Thunderstorm or Starfall for everyone on the server. It's a generous, social purchase ("PixelPaws summoned a Starfall!"). Aurora can never be bought, so Prismatic always stays special.

Guardrails: a monster can hold at most 2 mutations, and further rolls skip it. Economy dashboards track total coins per hour so mutation stacking can't inflate the economy unseen.

11

Social systems

Players want to play with friends more than anything else, and Roblox now tracks intentional co-play when recommending games. Every social feature here works for a player who is mostly idle.

Friends on server

+10% coins for each friend on the same server, up to +40%. A "Join a friend" button sits on the Welcome Back screen.

Ranch visits and petting

Walk onto any ranch and pet up to 20 monsters a day. The owner's monster gets +10 Mood and you get a Friendship Point. Ranches can be Liked, and the most-liked ranch on the server wears a crown.

Caravans

A co-op expedition for 2–4 players with +30% loot and a shared chest. You need friends or party members on the same server to start one.

Broadcasts

Epic-or-better hatches, hybrid discoveries, Huge weights and Aurora mutations are announced. Mythic and Rainbow hatches are announced across all servers.

Stampede: the server boss

  • Starts at :00 and :30 server time and lasts 90 seconds. Anyone joins with one tap from any screen.
  • Every participant's squad attacks automatically, and tapping Cheer adds up to +20% damage. Boss HP scales with the combined power of everyone taking part, so a full server always wins.
  • Rewards: every participant gets a Boss Chest (coins, food, Treats, 5 gems). Players with at least 5% of the damage have a 25% chance of a Stampede Egg. The top 3 get a Stampede Egg guaranteed.
  • The boss rotates through the four region bosses. Event seasons bring their own Stampede.

Trading

  • Unlocks at Ranch Lv 8 and after 30 minutes of total playtime, which blocks throwaway scam accounts.
  • Up to 8 items per side: monsters, eggs and tradable cosmetics. No currency.
  • Two steps: both players press Ready, then Confirm starts a 5-second countdown. Any change resets both sides and flashes the changed slot.
  • Every item shows the facts that set its value: rarity, variant, mutations, weight, level and stars. The game never shows an "official value", because players would game it.
  • Trades run on the server as a single transaction across both profiles, with a trade log (30 days) and support tools to roll one back.
  • Marketplace listings (asynchronous, coins only, 8% tax) arrive in update 1.3.

Leaderboards (weekly, per server and global)

Heaviest monster by species · Codex completion · Stampede damage · Hall of Fame count · Highest stage cleared. Global boards appear on a board in the Market Square, and the week's winners get a nameplate title for the following week.

12

Prestige

There are two layers. The first is soft and in the game from launch, so nobody loses anything. The second is a real reset that arrives in an update, once the most engaged players reach the cap.

Layer 1 · Hall of Fame (launch, Lv 20)

  • Retire an Adult at its level cap. It becomes a statue on your ranch that visitors can see, and an entry in the server's Hall of Fame.
  • You get a Legacy bonus of +1% to +6% coins (by rarity) for every monster of that element, stacking up to +60% per element, plus Stardust and Star Shards.
  • Stardust buys permanent upgrades: +1 incubator speed tier, +5% expedition loot, +1 daily breed, +15 min on jar caps, and more.

Layer 2 · Ranch Rebirth (update 1.4, Lv 40+)

  • Resets: coins, ranch buildings, region progress. Monsters you don't keep turn into Stardust at 50% of their value.
  • Keeps: Codex, Hall of Fame, gems, cosmetics, passes, and 3 Heirloom monsters (+1 per rebirth).
  • Gains: a Rebirth Star (×1.5 coins per star), a new ranch biome theme, the Celestial Egg tier, and a 3rd visible trait slot at 3 stars.
  • Always optional. The confirmation screen lists exactly what you keep and what you lose.

First session, second by second

The goal is to beat Roblox's most important early signal, first-play bounce. Something rewarding happens every few seconds in the first minute, and a new system appears every minute or two until minute 10.

  1. 0:00

    Spawn on your own plot. A starter egg is already wobbling in Incubator 1. One arrow, no text wall.

  2. 0:08

    The egg hatches into a guaranteed Uncommon baby (choose Cindlet, Bubblin or Mossbun). Name it. It walks into Pen 1.

  3. 0:20

    Its coin jar already shows +25. Tap to collect: the first number goes up. A quest bar appears: "Collect 100 coins".

  4. 0:45

    Quest reward: 300 coins. Arrow to the Egg Shop. Buy 2 Meadow Eggs (30 s hatch). The restock timer is pointed out once.

  5. 1:30

    Two hatches. Feed the starter 3 meals, then "Grow up" (tutorial growth time is 15 s). It becomes a Teen, a big moment with a sound sting.

  6. 2:30

    Expedition Gate: fight Whispering Meadow stage 1 with the 3 monsters (a scripted win), then stage 2, where the enemy is Sprout and the Ember choice shows the element advantage.

  7. 4:00

    Start a first 15-minute expedition. The game explains "it keeps going when you leave".

  8. 5:30

    Ranch Lv 3: Grove Egg and Incubator 2 unlock. Friends-on-server boost is explained if a friend is present.

  9. 7:00

    Scripted Rain (first session only). One monster is guaranteed to gain Soaked. This teaches pens + weather = value.

  10. 9:00

    Ranch Lv 4. If the Stampede is within 10 minutes, a countdown appears. If not, a solo tutorial boss plays instead. Players get their first boss chest and gems.

  11. 12:00

    The expedition returns: coins, food and a Grove Egg. Then the prompts: "Join our group for a free Crystal Egg code" and the favourite/notification ask, both after a win and never before.

Returning session: Welcome Back

The Welcome Back screen is the most-seen screen in the game. It adds up everything that happened offline, points to the next action, and offers the best rewarded-ad placement in the game.

Guardrails

  • Odds are shown for every random item. Paid random items are hidden for players whose PolicyService result has ArePaidRandomItemsRestricted. Coin eggs stay available.
  • Every species and rarity can be earned for free. Mythic has three free routes: Stampede Eggs, Royal Eggs and breeding.
  • No limited-time pressure on Robux items beyond a season. No "last chance" countdowns on paid offers.
  • All passes can be gifted, so players can buy for friends.
  • Monetization tuning waits until the game holds 200–300+ concurrent players. Before that, retention and discovery come first.

Tech architecture

Server services

  • ProfileService: session-locked DataStore profiles, schema version and migrations, autosave, BindToClose
  • RanchService: pens, jars, buildings, offline calculation on join
  • MonsterService: creation, growth, feeding, levels, stars, traits
  • IncubatorService, BreedingService: timestamp-based timers
  • ExpeditionService: deterministic battle simulation from a seed
  • WeatherService: per-server schedule, totems, mutation rolls
  • ShopService: global restock seeded by os.time() // 300
  • BossService, TradeService, SocialService
  • PurchaseService: ProcessReceipt with idempotent grants
  • AnalyticsService: funnel and economy events

Rules

  • The server owns everything. Remotes carry intent only (Egg.Buy(eggId), never amounts). Every call is validated for type and range and rate-limited per player.
  • No timer ever runs on the client. All timers are stored as startedAt plus duration and checked against os.time().
  • Offline calculation on join is capped at 7 days. It fills jars to their cap, completes timers and resolves expeditions from their seeds.
  • Content lives in ModuleScript config tables (species, eggs, regions, weather, traits). Adding content means adding data.
  • Luau numbers are fine here: late-game values stay under 1e15, far from double-precision limits.

Performance on phones

  • Monster rigs come from 8 shared body archetypes, each with one animation set (idle, walk, eat, happy, attack, hurt). Colour and parts are swapped at runtime.
  • At most 10 animated monsters per pen, 5 pens, and 6 ranches. Distant ranches use lower-detail models and slower animation updates. StreamingEnabled is on.
  • Battle replays and boss damage numbers are pooled. Weather particles scale down on low-end devices (UserGameSettings.SavedQualityLevel).
  • Target: 60 fps on mid-range phones, and 30 fps minimum on low-end phones with a full server.

Weekly live-ops rhythm

  • Every Saturday at 10:00 ET: update goes live, the rotating shop egg changes, and a new code drops on socials. That time works for both US mornings and EU afternoons.
  • Saturday hosted hour: developers join servers and trigger Starfall and Aurora on a schedule announced in advance. This worked for top idle games on Roblox.
  • Mid-week: a small balance patch, a leaderboard reset, and a teaser for the next update.
  • Every 6 weeks: a new Ranch Pass season (Season 6 opens Year 2; Seasons 7–14 follow through Dec 2028).
  • Every quarter: a new region opens on a Saturday. It ships one update early with an opening date (Regions.opensAt), so the launch day needs no code push.

Risks & mitigations

RiskMitigation
Duplication and trade scamsSession-locked profiles, trades that complete as one server transaction, trade logs, rollback tools, playtime and level gates, no currency trading
Economy inflation from stacked mutations2-mutation cap, strong coin sinks (eggs, breeding fees, marketplace tax), economy dashboards with weekly review
Art production load (78 forms)Modular kit of 8 archetypes with swappable parts, palette variants, a shared animation set per archetype
Crowded pet and collector genreLead with what others lack: evolution by diet, breeding hybrids and the expedition RPG. Put a hybrid in the thumbnail.
Phone performance with many monstersRender caps, lower-detail distant ranches, pooling, weather quality scaling, a weekly test on a low-end device
Scrutiny of spending by kidsNo paid timer skips, odds everywhere, PolicyService checks, free routes to Mythic, no pressure countdowns
Roblox's 20-minute idle kickOffline progress doesn't depend on staying connected. Idle is the game, and leaving is fine.
Content runs outContent is data. Plan a 3-month runway before launch and ship updates every 3 weeks.
