## TL;DR

1. **Idle is proven at the top of Roblox** — Grow a Garden and Steal a Brainrot, the two biggest games in platform history, are idle games at heart.
2. **RPGs and simulators retain best**, so an idle RPG sits where the two best-retaining genres meet.
3. **Play on phones first**: ~72% of sessions are on mobile.
4. **Social is the #1 thing players want.** Make the game GUI-first, not GUI-only: put a small shared 3D hub around the menus.
5. **Must-haves:** offline progress, layered prestige, co-op server events, and a first minute where something good happens every few seconds.
6. **Sell convenience, speed and cosmetics.** Don't sell pay-to-skip timers or power over other players, and always show odds on random items.
7. **Top idle RPG picks:** *Monster Ranch Idle* (widest audience) and *Guildhall Idle* (best classic idle RPG). *Endless Tower Idle* is the fastest to ship.
